      02 Sentiment

      Hand it a review.
      Get the reasoning.

      A hybrid engine: VADER polarity fused with a product-review rule set of 122 phrases and 45 keywords. Every verdict shows its working — the exact language that moved the score, marked in the reviewer's own text.

      03 Facets

      What exactly
      are they judging?

      Reviews rarely praise or damn a whole product. They praise the battery and damn the screen. Twenty-eight extracted aspects, each with its own polarity split.

      Bars show polarity composition · figures show mention volume and net sentiment

      07 Summary

      Long review in.
      The gist out.
